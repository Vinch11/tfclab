## B_BIKE_VO2_3MIN

**Contexte documentaire proposé.** La configuration travail/récupération influence les réponses aiguës des intervalles ; ce contexte ne démontre ni l'optimalité ni l'admissibilité individuelle de cette recette.

**Recette relue.** 20 min progressive + 3×30 s Z5 r60 s; main 5–7×3 min Z5 r3 min Z1–Z2; 10 min cool-down. The final rest policies remain unresolved in the original and candidate in V19.

**Étude effectivement examinée au niveau du résumé.** Narrative programming review with examples from several sports; no single intervention cohort or athlete-specific dose established. Review of work/relief intensity and duration, modality, repetitions, series and between-series recovery, plus cardiopulmonary and non-cardiopulmonary loads.

**Transfert non établi.** No direct validation of 5–7×3 min, Z5 individual domain, 3-min recovery, final-rest occurrence, weekly frequency or VLamax-based restriction. Existing source restrictions remain unchanged.

**Données nécessaires.** sport-specific upper-intensity anchor and uncertainty; actual recovery tolerance; microcycle interaction; observed response and reason for limiter priority.

**Statut :** brouillon de contexte familial, dose non vérifiée individuellement, sans revue experte humaine.

## C_IM_AERO_HOLD

**Contexte documentaire proposé.** La position sur le vélo peut modifier le confort, la fatigue et la douleur ressentis ; cette étude ne valide ni les blocs de 15 minutes ni leur équivalence aux blocs de 10 minutes.

**Recette relue.** 4×15 min Z2 en position aéro, r5 min Z1; unknown original final-rest occurrence; candidate totals 75 or 80 min. Source avoid: lumbar pain. The archived 6×10 alternative has another continuous exposure.

**Étude effectivement examinée au niveau du résumé.** 20 cyclists; results about perceptions across tested positions, not an individual Ironman cohort tolerance certificate. Three tests in randomized order, 45 min at 50% individual peak aerobic power, with different knee and trunk flexion combinations.

**Transfert non établi.** Study intensity is relative to peak aerobic power, not automatically Z2/FTP. No 15-min bout tolerance, recovery duration, aerodynamic gain or equivalence of interruptions is demonstrated.

**Données nécessaires.** actual postural exposure and cervical/lumbar symptoms; bike-fit and position-specific power anchor; response to interruptions and full microcycle; future reassessment before any increase.

**Statut :** brouillon de contexte familial, dose non vérifiée individuellement, sans revue experte humaine.
