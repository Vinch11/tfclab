# Plan architecture
- Reconciliation and validation share the time-weighted demanding classifier and beginner cap; established athletes exceeding the review reference retain their sessions with a domain-aware warning, while recovery/calendar checks remain independent. Why: a universal count conflated controlled work and severe intensity.
- Resolve marathon-B preparation through dated next-race pace context without promoting B to an A peak; reviewed dual-goal cards and protected taper reminders pass existing catalogue eligibility. Why: final-goal quotas and rotation obscured marathon preparation and taper coverage.

- Apply `longRidePolicy` to the engine-owned global phase calendar before existing level/injury floor reductions; transport the same dedicated-bike floor to prompt, repairs and final validation. Why: brick totals cannot certify bike duration and regenerated windows must not restart phase progress.
- Weekly endurance budgets exclude strength; preserve total-time and strength-aware calendar, historical-load and recovery checks. Why: excluding lifting from endurance availability must not make physiological load disappear.
- Display weekly working time and separate strength session counts through `weeklyTimeBreakdown`; missing strength duration must not block endurance budgets. Why: lifting duration is not a reliable weekly time measure.
- Shared IM text correction applies only to explicitly identified Ironman blocks and references tfclDoctrine; exports preserve the delivered prescription without catalogue examples. Why: standalone marathon pace and reference annexes contradicted the actual Ironman session.
- Generation preparation carries marathon reentry ceilings, sport-separated nutrition provenance and global strength/dose continuity instructions; unknown tolerance and lifting experience stay unknown. Why: independent chunk examples cannot establish recovery or authorize advanced loading.
- Resolve Auto hours and displayed references through `recommendedWeeklyRange`; carry both endpoints into the existing level-adjusted weekly budget and retain initial-load review. Explicit coach hours stay authoritative, uncovered objectives have no inferred hours, and StartToRun retains its safety envelope. Why: a hidden matrix midpoint must not contradict the displayed reference or certify individual tolerance.
- Coach-entered weekly hours become a progressive endurance target (85→100 % before taper) with a minimum; pre-race taper weeks are capped at 2 and race-proximity cards are checked against the actual race day. Why: plateaued volume and early J-1 cards contradicted coach settings.
- Keep raw volume/calendar validation available for deterministic repairs, but project delivery findings through `planAdvisoryPolicy`; save, adaptation and viewer use that projection. Why: raw severity must not become an action refusal or silently remove an alert.
- Resolve dated competition protections through shared `raceCalendarProtection`, transport them into quotas/prompts, apply the final calendar repair after day-moving passes, and independently revalidate assembled plans. Why: an existing race on the wrong day and B-race weeks bypassed date, taper and recovery checks; local windows are not terminal plans.
- Treat secondary continuous races as short tapers, preserve dated marathon reminders and pre-race load references, and stage marathon reentry across three weeks; preserve dedicated Ironman long-effort reminders before race week. Why: day safety alone allowed excessively empty recovery followed by a full Build restart.
- Recheck full-plan race/calendar and coach-load contracts at every client save/export boundary, including Markdown and batch generation; remote session writes receive the source plan and DET checks new writes separately from receipt verification. Why: selecting one week/session or moving an export date must not bypass trajectory safety or invalidate settled receipts.
- Volume, race-calendar and week checks are never refusals at generation, assembly, adaptation, save or export: they surface as visible warnings in `finalValidationIssues` and a toast. Why: the coach decides; refusals hid whole plans.

- Preserve VO2max and VMA as distinct ranked coach priorities in plan coverage, explicitly sharing their aerobic stimulus without duplicating session totals; latest page order overrides a restored coach payload. Why: family deduplication and stale policies hid the coach's L2.
- Catalogue duration deviations are warnings in final plan validation, while incompatible sport, phase, intensity and structure retain their content checks. Why: a volume adjustment must not be treated as a fundamental goal/content conflict.
- Explicit strength frequency and double-day limits are carried in weekly quota contracts shared by layout, prevalidation and final validation; run-cycle maintenance adds only selected sports without removing run targets. Why: prompt-only choices contradicted imposed calendars.
- All generation paths preserve the selected start date, even without an active plan, and update the A race contract; stored limiter policies cannot override visible frequency. Why: silent resets shortened plans.
- Terrain eligibility uses explicit catalogue metadata and the prescribed day in shared checks, with terrain carried to final validation; B/C races remain tune-ups rather than independent peaks. Why: available terrain and race priorities must not remain prompt-only preferences.
- Session texts are personalized only at display/export through `renderIntensities.enrichWithAbsoluteValues` (screen and PDF share the viewer's target table): the prescribed % FTP/% VMA is authoritative and a contradicting zone label is relabelled on the athlete's grid; triathlon race-pace % FTP is replaced by `racePowerRange` (raceBikePower); bricks read watts before T2 and paces after. Why: catalogue cards use the old 6-zone vocabulary and a generic IM %, which contradicted the athlete's zones, Garmin and simulation.
- In the final IM/70.3 cycle's peak (specific) weeks, at most one threshold/VO2max session per week (`enforceSpecificPhaseIntensity`, race-pace and marathon-pace sessions excluded); the excess is replaced in catalogue (race pace/endurance, same sport and duration) and the session text says so. The established-athlete rule allowing more than three demanding sessions elsewhere is unchanged (coach decision 27/11/2026). Why: specific weeks stacked VO2max and threshold work instead of race-pace endurance.
- Final-race taper weeks keep one shortened long session (`applyTaperLongSessionFloors`: IM ride 180/120 min and run 75/60 min at 2/1 weeks out; 70.3, marathon and half-marathon equivalents), capped by the longest easy taper card eligible that week, before level/injury individualization; in taper weeks a card without a same-intent replacement falls back to an eligible easy/race-pace taper card of the same sport. Why: the longest ride fell from 5h40 to 1h15 three weeks before an Ironman, then a floor longer than any taper card blocked the save.
- After an intermediate marathon in a triathlon plan, the protected recovery weeks keep a minimum of easy bike/swim sessions (`postMarathonCrossTrainingMinimum`: 2 bike + 2 swim at J+1–7, 3 bike + 2 swim at J+8–14), never a mandatory run; rest-only days and per-session caps are unchanged. Why: the plan dropped to 1 h then 2h45 before jumping back to 14 h.
- Races registered in the athlete's fiche that fall inside the plan but are missing from it trigger a warning at generation; the coach adds them through the intermediate-objective button, never automatically (dedicated add button removed, coach decision 27/11/2026). Why: a registered marathon was absent from an Ironman plan while the report showed it.
- Race-proximity cards (`isRaceProximityCard`: J-3, eve or morning of the race…) are never chosen as repair candidates outside a race week, and any such card left outside a race week is replaced (same intent, else easy/race-pace of the same sport) by `repairRaceProximityOutsideRaceWeek`, mirroring the save check. Why: misplaced openers made saves impossible.
